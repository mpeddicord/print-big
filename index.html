Print Big

Turn any picture into a big poster on a normal printer. Pick the finished size, download the PDF, print every page at 100%, then cut, overlap and tape.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, SVG, or a photo straight from your phone. It never leaves your device.

Putting it together

  1. Print all pages at 100% / Actual size, never "Fit to page" or "Shrink to fit".
  2. On the assembly sheet, measure the square: it must be exactly 1 inch (2.54 cm). If not, fix the print scale before cutting anything.
  3. Lay the pages out by row and column, following the map on the assembly sheet.
  4. Page 1 stays whole. Every other page: cut along its dashed edge(s), lay it over the neighbouring page so the picture lines up, and tape it. Small ticks in the margins show where the next page's edge should land.
